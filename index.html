Briefing

Useful Tips

Useful Tips

Ops
  • Performance is mostly GPU — Adreno 610, Adreno 613, Adreno 619, Mali G57 MCX, Mali G68 MCX/MPX, Mali G610 MCX/MPX, Mali G615 MCX/MPX, Mali G720 MCX/MPX, Adreno 620, Adreno 710, Adreno 720, Adreno 722, Adreno 810 or better recommended for realtime gameplay. Phones, smartwatches, smart TV, PC, laptop and more are supported. (needs a browser)
  • Play in fullscreen mode (F11) for an immersive experience.

Mouse

  • Middle mouse button to drag the simulation area around.
  • Mouse wheel to zoom in and out — or to change brush size while holding down B.
  • Left mouse button to use selected tool. Hold down Ctrl (command on mac) to perform the inverted action.
